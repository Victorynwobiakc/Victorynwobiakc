How Duck works

  1. Draw on the canvas, then tap ＋ in the timeline to add the next frame.
  2. The faded red drawing is the previous frame (onion skin) — trace and change it a little.
  3. Tap ▶ to play. Change speed in ⋮ → Canvas settings.
  4. Tap the selected frame again for duplicate / move / delete.
  5. Tap Layer to add layers (e.g. background + character).
  6. Share exports a video (with your audio). Everything autosaves in this browser.
